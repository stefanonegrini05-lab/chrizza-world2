# Chrizza's World2 ♡

A personal, romantic website made with love, dedicated to Chrizza and her cat Tortoro in Bugo (Cagayan de Oro).

## ✨ Features
- **Romantic & Full Design:** Warm tropical/rose pastel atmosphere with smooth glassmorphism effects and floating ambient details.
- **Our Story & Timeline:** From that random night on OmeTV with the white T-shirt to 11 months and counting[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).
- **Monthsaries & Letters:** A complete digital archive of monthly notes and heartfelt letters written from month 2 to month 10[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).
- **Tortoro's World (Minigames):**
  1. *Bugo Cozy House:* Take care of Tortoro, feed him snacks, and give him sweet pets[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).
  2. *Tortoro's Heart Hunt:* An arcade runner adventure where Tortoro collects hearts and avoids obstacles[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
- **Love Quiz:** Fun trivia to test memories about your relationship.
