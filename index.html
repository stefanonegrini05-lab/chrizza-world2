Our Story

Two people, two countries, a story started by chance and turned into home.

01

One night, a dark room, and a way-too-bright shirt

We met on OmeTV. You were in a dark room at night, already ready to go to sleep. On my side, however, it was early afternoon. I was wearing a white shirt so bright it probably blinded you: practically a walking lightbulb, haha. I managed to see your face for only two seconds, but sometimes a few moments are all it takes for something to begin.
02

First chats and my awkward moments

We started talking about our hobbies, and at some point, you asked me how old I was. With embarrassing naturalness, I lied and told you we were the same age: actually, I'm a year older. I also told you my favorite movie was The Hangover and that I went to the gym. To prove it, I thought it was a great idea to flex my bicep in front of the camera. Yes, I know: a very ungracious and decidedly cringe gesture. 😂
03

A Reel, a message, and the feeling of being heard

I gave you my Instagram and, two days later, you were the first to write to me, sending me a Reel. It was a different approach than what I was used to and that's exactly why it intrigued me. You told me that, despite being Italian, I looked Mexican; and above all, you watched the movie I recommended. That little gesture made me feel heard. It wasn't just a conversation: you were giving importance to the things I told you.
04

December 18th

Just two weeks after that first encounter, on December 18th, I found the courage to ask you to become my girlfriend. From an acquaintance born almost by chance, something had begun that, without knowing it, would change our days and our way of looking at the future.
05

From two shy people to everyday life together

In the beginning, we were super shy. Even making a video call seemed difficult: we had so many things to say, but sometimes we didn't even know where to start. Today, instead, we call each other every day and fall asleep together on video call. What used to make us embarrassed has become one of the best parts of our routine. Ever since then, we've loved each other madly. And this, Chrizza, is just our story continuing.

📚 A tiny grammar lesson

Essere — to be

Io sono = I am · Tu sei = You are · Lui/Lei è = He/She is · Noi siamo = We are · Voi siete = You are · Loro sono = They are.

Avere — to have

Io ho = I have · Tu hai = You have · Lui/Lei ha = He/She has · Noi abbiamo = We have · Voi avete = You have · Loro hanno = They have.

Many adjectives ending in -o are masculine and those in -a feminine: bello / bella, carino / carina. To negate a sentence, put non before the verb: “Ti amo” → “Non ti amo”. (But we prefer the first! 😋)

🗣️ Pronunciation tips

CH has a hard sound like K in front of E or I: “Chi” sounds like “kee”.
CI / CE sound like “chee / cheh”: “Ciao” is pronounced “chow”.
GN is similar to “ny”: lasagna.
GLI is a soft sound: famiglia.
Double consonants: the consonant is pronounced longer; pala and palla are different.
